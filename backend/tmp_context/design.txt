Carbon Horizon
Learn Your Impact. Shape the Future.

Version 1.0




1. Design Vision
Carbon Horizon should feel like:


"Google Earth + Tesla Dashboard + Sustainability Intelligence Platform"


The experience should communicate:


     • Environmental awareness
     • Data intelligence
     • Future forecasting
     • Actionable sustainability
     • Trust and credibility

The platform should not look like a school project.


It should feel like a modern climate-tech startup product.
