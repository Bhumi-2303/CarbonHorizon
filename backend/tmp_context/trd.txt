Carbon Horizon
Learn Your Impact. Shape the Future.

Version: 2.0




1. Executive Summary
Carbon Horizon is an AI-powered Personal Sustainability Intelligence Platform that enables users to
measure, understand, simulate, forecast, and reduce their carbon footprint through scientifically backed
calculations, AI-driven sustainability guidance, and long-term behavioral tracking.


This document defines the technical architecture, system requirements, APIs, database standards, security
requirements, deployment strategy, and implementation guidelines for the Carbon Horizon MVP.




2. Technical Objectives
The platform shall:


     • Calculate user carbon footprints accurately
     • Identify major emission contributors
     • Generate AI-powered sustainability recommendations
     • Simulate future lifestyle scenarios
     • Forecast future emissions
     • Track sustainability goals
     • Monitor eco-friendly habits
     • Store historical user progress
     • Support cloud deployment and future scaling

4. Architectural Principles
Principle 1
Business logic must reside in backend services.


No carbon calculations shall occur in frontend applications.




Principle 2
AI services must never generate carbon calculations.


AI shall only:


      • Explain results
      • Provide recommendations
      • Generate action plans
      • Answer sustainability questions

All numerical calculations must originate from backend services.




Principle 3
All carbon calculations must be traceable.


Each emission result must be linked to:


      • Calculation version
      • Emission factor version
      • Data source




Principle 4
System must support future feature expansion without database redesign.

Module 3: Carbon Calculation Engine

Purpose

Convert activity data into carbon emissions.


Formula

Carbon Emission = Activity Data × Emission Factor


Example

Transport

= 19.2 kg CO₂e
