Carbon Horizon
Learn Your Impact. Shape the Future.

Version: 1.0




1. Application Overview
Carbon Horizon helps users understand, simulate, and reduce their carbon footprint through AI-powered
insights, forecasting, and sustainability planning.


The application flow is designed to guide users from awareness to measurable carbon reduction.
