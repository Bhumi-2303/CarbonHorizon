Success Criteria
The project will be considered successful if:


         • Users can calculate their carbon footprint.
         • AI generates personalized sustainability guidance.
         • Simulations demonstrate measurable reduction opportunities.
         • Forecasts visualize future environmental impact.
         • Users track eco-friendly habits.
         • The application is deployed successfully on Google Cloud.
         • The complete workflow can be demonstrated in under 5 minutes during evaluation.
